Who goes on the list

Students in a group always go on the list, whichever rule you pick.

Check-ins

Each check-in is due that many school days after they were last seen. After the last one they leave the check-in list for good, unless they start declining.

U codes (Attendance Manager)

Uses the U column in the attendance file, which is a running total. The app subtracts the total on the start date to count U codes since then.

Groups

Automatic groups fill themselves from each day's figures, in every year group. Manual groups are the VP's choice: add students from the Students table (tick them, then "Add to group"), from a student's panel, or with Add from a list (paste names or choose a spreadsheet).

Rewards

Staff pick one of these when they log a reward. "From" is the lowest milestone it can be given for.

Staff roles

Learning leaders and pastoral leaders see their own year group. Everyone else is a tutor: they're matched to their form from the staff codes below, or pick one if they aren't in the list.

Cover for absent staff

If someone is off for a while, share their students between other staff. Each student is given to one person, by percentage, and stays with them until the cover ends. The students show up in that person's lists marked Covering. A Learning or Pastoral Leader's year can go to other leaders; a tutor's form can go to other tutors.

Staff codes

Upload the staff code list (Staff Code, Full Name) as the Word file, a spreadsheet, or paste it below. Tutors are matched to the forms with their code in the name, e.g. 10BRN or 11HLC/PLN, using emails worked out from the name (Mrs J Smith → j.smith@rainford.org.uk). Tutors who aren't matched pick from the forms that have no tutor.

Timetable

Upload both Synergy exports: the student timetable (Full Name, Year Group, Reg Group, Class 1, Class 2…) and the whole-school period timetable. Choose both at once, or one after the other. Only needed when the timetable changes.

Synergy import

The app can fetch the Tracker-Milestones report from Synergy by itself every school morning, so nobody has to upload it. The password is stored in the script's private settings and never shown again.

Upload data

Upload the tracker export (StudentId, LastName, FirstName, YearGroup, RegGroup, SessionsPresent, SessionsPossible, PP, U). Choose the Excel file, or copy the rows with the headings and paste them here. Sessions are halved to give days, and FSM is used as DA. Set "Correct up to" to the date the figures are from.
